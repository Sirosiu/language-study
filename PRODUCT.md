# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
Confirmed formats: interactive course plus PDFs and Obsidian. Implementation choice: static HTML, CSS and JavaScript deployed through GitHub Pages, with Node.js build and verification scripts.
## Users
Sirosiu reads English and Hindi and is studying 15 named languages. Previous reference-book formatting was difficult to learn from.
## Product Purpose
Teach alphabet and script, sound, word formation, grammar, vocabulary, reading and speaking through connected lessons and practice.
## Operating Context
Self-paced study across phones, tablets and computers, no deadline. Progress transfers through manual export/import. Language packs can be saved offline per device. English explanations. PDFs and Obsidian were requested in the linked Language chat. Arabic has separate MSA, Egyptian, Levantine and Gulf material.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Only Arabic, Danish, Dutch, English, Finnish, French, German, Georgian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Mandarin, Russian, Spanish and Turkish.
At least 1000 grammar questions and around 1000 vocabulary entries per language. Translations and explained answers. Audio where available.
One consolidated hub/note per language. Keep lessons, questions and answers together; no repeated 01/02 files. More content with fewer containers.
## Product Principles
Teach before testing. One rule at a time. Native writing with pronunciation help. Explain why an answer works. Keep lessons and practice directly connected.
## Evidence on Hand
Original Language chat and original source lessons. New language data must identify its dictionary sources and licenses.
